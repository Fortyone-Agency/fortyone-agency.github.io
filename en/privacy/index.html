Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information

Fortyone Agency LLC ("we") treats the protection of personal information as a core responsibility and handles it according to the policy set out below.

1. Information we collect

We collect the name, email address, company name, and enquiry details you provide through our contact form. This website does not use cookies for tracking, and we run no analytics tools.

2. How we use it

We use the information solely to respond to your enquiry, clarify what you are asking for, and communicate about the provision of our services. We do not use it for any other purpose.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or disclose personal information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

4. External services

Our contact form is provided by Google Forms, so submissions are stored on servers operated by Google LLC. This website also loads Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google LLC as part of delivering those fonts. Information handled by these services is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

5. Retention

We keep information no longer than necessary to fulfil the purposes above, and delete it appropriately once it is no longer needed.

6. Access, correction and deletion

If you ask us to disclose, correct, suspend the use of, or delete your personal information, we will verify your identity and respond within a reasonable period. Please get in touch via the contact form.

7. Contact

For any questions about this policy, please get in touch via the contact form.
